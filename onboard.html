Commerce Assistant

Set up your assistant

Two steps: connect your Shopify store, then connect the WhatsApp number your customers message. Your assistant starts in review mode — it drafts replies for you to check and sends nothing until you switch it live.

1Connect your store

Grants read-only access to your orders, products and fulfillments so the assistant can answer order-status and product questions.

2Connect WhatsApp

Connect the number you already use in the WhatsApp Business app. You keep using the app on your phone as normal — the assistant works alongside it, and steps back whenever you reply yourself.

No WhatsApp Business app? Connect a new number instead

You're all set. Your assistant is connected and running in review mode. We'll be in touch to review its drafts with you before switching it live.
