Data Deletion

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Before you publish: replace the contact email with your real address.

You can request deletion of the personal information Commerce Assistant holds about you.

Merchants

To delete your account and all associated data — your configuration, connected credentials, contacts, messages and run logs — email privacy@your-domain.example from your account email with the subject "Delete my account". We remove the data within 30 days, except where we must retain limited records to meet legal obligations.

End customers

If you messaged a business that uses Commerce Assistant and want your conversation data deleted, contact that business (the data controller). You may also email us at privacy@your-domain.example with the phone number you messaged from and the business name, and we will delete the messages and contact record we hold for you, and assist the business with the request.

What gets deleted

Messages, contact records, personalisation data and run logs tied to the request. We may retain minimal, anonymised or legally required records (for example, billing records) as permitted by law.

Timeframe

We action verified deletion requests within 30 days and confirm by email.
