Commerce Assistant

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Commerce Assistant ("we", "us", "the Service") is operated by Lyra Distribution Corp. We provide an AI customer-support and sales assistant that answers e-commerce merchants' customers over messaging channels (such as WhatsApp), on the merchant's behalf, using the merchant's own store data.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to two groups:

  • Merchants — the businesses that subscribe to the Service and connect their store and messaging account.
  • End customers — the people who message a merchant's connected number and receive replies from the assistant.

Where we handle end customers' information, we do so as a processor acting on the instructions of the merchant, who is the controller of that data.

3. How and why we use it

  • To generate and send automated support and sales replies on the merchant's behalf, grounded in the merchant's store data and recorded facts.
  • To look up a customer's own orders and verify ownership before revealing order details.
  • To hand a conversation to the merchant's staff when a human is needed.
  • To operate, secure, debug and improve the Service.

We do not use end customers' message content for advertising, and we do not sell personal information.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users or the Service.

5. Platform Data from Meta

Information we receive through the WhatsApp Business Platform ("Platform Data") is used solely to provide the messaging assistant to the merchant whose account it belongs to. We do not sell Platform Data, use it for advertising, or transfer it to data brokers, and we handle it in accordance with Meta's terms and policies.

6. Retention

We retain messages, contacts and run logs for as long as the merchant's account is active, and for a limited period afterwards as needed for security, dispute resolution and legal compliance, after which they are deleted or anonymised. A merchant may request earlier deletion (see section 8).

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to stored data is restricted to the Service's backend using least-privilege, server-side credentials; there is no public access to tenant data. Per-tenant secrets (such as store access tokens) are stored securely and never exposed to other tenants or end users.

8. Your rights & how to request access or deletion

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. End customers should contact the merchant they messaged (the controller); we will assist that merchant in fulfilling the request. To make a request directly, or for deletion, see our Data Deletion page or email the contact below.

9. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours. Where required, such transfers are made under appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses).

10. Children

The Service is for businesses and is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, take reasonable steps to notify merchants.
